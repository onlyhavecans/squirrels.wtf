## Summary

- Short Stream Sunday
    - I got back into it and was so happy to, but the persistent post-lumbar puncture headache makes it hard to sit at a screen and focus on a game and be entertaining for any long time.
- The end of the shell wars
    - In the end, I am never happy with a heavyweight shell, and even lightweight ones. I rolled back and then cleaned up my configs a touch, finally got waybar to play nice… er. I swear it always comes back to me being annoyed at waybar.
    - But the next version looks like it fixes most of the things I wished worked. So since I use NixOS, I patched it in and am now using the latest version from the main branch. And after some restyling, it's pretty great.
- The eternity of head pain
    - I also had to lean on my nausea meds a bunch this week.
    - Feeling all messed up
- The lowest-key birthday I ever had
    - I normally like to throw out as hard as I can for my birthday. However, I am feeling so wrecked and headachy I just can't.
- 1Password -> Bitwarden
    - Yep, this is called voting with my money.
    - The migration is anything but painless with a vault that runs back to the low 2000s. I got all my passkeys and passwords over, but it didn't respect the archive vs. active status, so I had to go through and re-archive EVERYTHING I have archived in the past: about 600 items.
    - The SSH key functionality was easy peasy, not as nice as 1Password's, but once I archived years of old keys, it worked well. I liked being able to select which keys to use from 1Password, but it hasn't been a blocker for me yet.
    - Bitwarden is a bit awkward. Not bad, but awkward. There is no sort by used, created, changed, nothing. Only name. Weird and gross. Also, the browser plugin and client are not linked in any way, so it's two separate unlocks and stores.
    - Bitwarden ALSO does not have an `op` equivalent OR a quick viewer, both of which I think are pretty bad. Luckily, the open-source Rust client `rwb` works as an op CLI replacement and, with some scripting, a quick viewer.
    - There are some rough edges in Bitwarden for sure, but none of them are dealbreakers once I got `rbw` working and integrated it into some stuff.
    - Still **feels wild** to delete an account for a service I have been paying for for 17 years now.
- ALL the planning calls
    - CAR-T starts immediately after Labor Day weekend. And I found out about this at the begining of the week. So it's been a SCRAMBLE to get everything booked and ready to be gone for TWO WHOLE MONTHS.
    - Also, like, wtf, it's been a mess, off, on, off, on, maybe… ok no wait we got you.
    - Luckily I got my retired mom to help, and I got some assistance for booking out the stay. It's going to be months.
