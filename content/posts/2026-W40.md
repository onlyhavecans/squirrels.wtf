## Summary

- Proton-ing
    - This is something overdue. Back in 2024, I tried Proton but couldn't get past having separate calendar and mail clients. Now that I am at the tail end of prying myself off of Apple, it's plenty of time to give up on that dream, really. It seems like no other ecosystem really caters to that the way iOS does.
    - Luckily I still have a discounted rate for it
    - The first part of the migration was an afternoon ordeal: set up validation records, wait, change over records, set up email forwarding, import all the things… wait a day, clean up all the old DNS records and domain setups in iCloud…
    - The next phase will be very time-consuming. After waiting a few days, I can slog through migrating everything that wasn't going to my vanity name, but to iCloud directly. OOof. Updating your email every 20 years is a good idea, right?
- Key rotation time!
    - This is a pretty rote procedure for me, but I like to rotate out my SSH keys every two years. A regular reminder that credential rotation matters for safety, and once you build habits (and documentation), it can happen with little effort.
    - Work is pushing for yearly rotations of all personal credentials, but since it's not that much effort, I'm not bothered by the idea of moving up to yearly.
- Utah Week 4
    - More rain?! Gasp. Yes. Still don't love driving in the torrential pour, but it's not really bad here. Most people know how to drive in the rain.
    - 0800 Monday morning: old line out, addressing the wound, and a fresh new Utah-style line. I already miss my old line, but I will adjust to it. I will not adjust, however, to having my entire forearm coated in multiple layers of iodine. It's like glue.
    - Also learned my trip is being extended by two more weeks. Boooooo
    - And my first night with the new line was a _trainwreck._ My heart was having a cranky, and nobody likes any of that, so I got almost no sleep. After X-rays and multiple conversations, I was told. If it's making you uncomfortable… don't do it. Ugh.
    - Yay, good night's sleep! And took mom home! And I got amazing news: my visits were cut down, and I was cleared to go home! And then the night's sleep was absolutely amazing, heavy and long.
    - It's weird how free and easy I feel.
- Working workwork
    - I still feel like I am in fits and starts, but I am getting way more output than before now.
- A reach out means a lot
    - A friend poked me in the DMs that they really like seeing my posts pop up in their RSS feed. 💜 It really made my day.
    - I turned off all comments and reactions on the actual blog, and even disabled all my vanity analytics. I wanted to blog about what I blog about without random numbers pushing me toward this or that thing. I don't need to optimize my posting; maybe do it more 😄
    - This does mean when people reach out, it means all the more.
- Going on stupid walks for my stupid health
    - Without my mom around and with fewer appointments, I decided I gotta do something about how sore I am getting. My leg muscles are tight and hurt so bad when my mobility is reduced.
    - A nice 30-minute walk away is a basement coffee shop. The coffee wasn't mind-blowing, and they didn't have any pastries when I got there, but it was still a great lil local joint I need to come by when I want to try more of their menu.
- It got better then… maybe worse?
    - My rash started clearing up on Thursday a lot, but I think I actually went overboard with the steroid cream, and Friday it was bad. In fact, it was so bad that after walking about 2 miles, I found another open sore under the edge of my new bandage on my new PICC already. I don't understand why I can't win here.
- Sleepiest Saturday
