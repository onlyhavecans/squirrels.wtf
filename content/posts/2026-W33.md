## Music

- Rob Zombie
    - One of the big classic scary man say spooky things acts. I never really kept up with him since "Past, Present & Future", so the car trip is a good time to get caught up and see what he has been up to. I picked up a lot of the new albums and listened through most of them, trying to figure out which ones really stick, and which went through the wash.
    - I do have to say it really feels like it's been going downhill since the White Zombie breakup. There were some huge initial projects, and then a lot of… mid. That said, there are still a few bangers throughout.

    - When you think of "spooky man say scary thing" it's hard not to put Manson first. Sadly, he's also the top of the list when I think of musicians who are drug-addled shitbags.
