- Proton Continued
    - Now that I have everything linked up to Proton Mail and calendar and vpn again. I still have the same complaints about mail and calendar since you really can't use a 3rd party client at all, and that's more important than the mail.
    - One of the things I ruminated on a lot was how much Bitwarden is not checking my boxes. It's kinda almost somewhat there, but it's also very not. And when I first tried Proton in 2024, it was brand new and rudimentary, to be generous.
    - But now, two years later, it's deeply superior to Bitwarden. It has far more templates and item types like 1P has, but Bitwarden lacks them. It also works R/W offline, unlike Bitwarden. It also has better sorts, multi-select, and a good first-party CLI that works a lot like `op`.
    - I migrated, and I am really excited so far. The CLI client is slow IMO, but I found some fixes that replace lots of lookups. But the UX on every inch of it is better.
    - However, manually replacing all my passkeys is pretty brutal.
    - Proton Pass isn't flawless in its UX, but it's still damn better than Bitwarden, and with a smidge of fixing up a lot of items from the Bitwarden import and a small bit of tweaks, I think I can get it up to where 1Password was for me.
    - There is a lot of cleanup in Proton Pass that I'd like to do, but I'm going to slowly try to build through that since it's going to be a lot of manual work.
    - There will also be a long tail of cleanup on mail since there are so many things to switch over to my other addresses and old email to clean out.

- The death of a travel router
    - The GL-iNet Slate AX I have been using for almost three years regularly seems to be giving up the ghost. I think it should last much longer than that, but I have also had Wi-Fi gear fail routinely after three to four years.
    - Either way, it seems that the fan inside it has frozen, and it's rebooting and losing wifi a lot. I could dig it apart and fix it, but I don't have the space, time, or tools to do that while on a trip. How cursed is Utah?! Two important devices failing on me in one trip.
    - I decided to get an upgrade. I am very impressed with the Beryl 7 so far. It supports a much higher-level firmware and performs very well. It has MLO for 6E and 7 and is a good bit heavier. The touchscreen is a great addition, so I can quickly switch between presets and see the status of a lot of important stats, including login QR codes for its networks. The only bummer is it's a smidge bigger and a lot heavier; the heat sink(s) must be massive compared to the Slate AX
- The riskiest move I can make

    - Then I took it to another level. I moved my VPN from a rPi running WireGuard into the home network, up to my router running WireGuard. This took a while because it was hard to juggle connections for testing, and the WireGuard setup isn't 100% clear since you don't just set up that section; you also need to manually set up all the firewall rules for it. Not a big strain but interesting.
    - I bet this will improve my WireGuard performance if only a bit. That rPi has a lot of different duties, and it's half as powerful as my router on a good day. Oh, also fewer hops and no taking everything down to a smaller pipe.
